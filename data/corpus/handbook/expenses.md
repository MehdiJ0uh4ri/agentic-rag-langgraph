# Expense Policy

## Scope
This policy covers all employees and contractors submitting expenses through Concur.
It was last revised on 2025-11-04 and supersedes the 2023 edition.

## Approval thresholds
Expenses under EUR 250 are auto-approved once a receipt is attached. Expenses between
EUR 250 and EUR 2,000 require line-manager approval. Anything above EUR 2,000 requires
approval from both the line manager and the cost-centre owner. Approvals must be granted
within five working days; unapproved claims older than 30 days are closed automatically.

## Travel
Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class is permitted only for
flights whose scheduled duration exceeds six hours, and must be booked at least fourteen
days in advance. Rail travel is reimbursed at standard fare; first class rail requires no
special approval on journeys over three hours.

## Per diem
The per diem for domestic travel is EUR 45 per full day and EUR 22 for a partial day.
International per diem rates are set per country in the Finance wiki and are not
reproduced here.

## Non-reimbursable
Fines, in-flight wifi above EUR 15 per flight, personal entertainment, and alcohol
outside a client-facing meal are not reimbursable.
