# Leave and Working Time

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing at
2.083 days per completed month. Up to 5 unused days may be carried into the next year and
must be used before 31 March, after which they lapse.

## Notice
Leave of five consecutive days or fewer requires seven days' notice. Longer absences
require 21 days' notice. Leave during the two weeks around a major release freeze is
granted only with director approval.

## Parental leave
Statutory parental leave follows local law in each operating country. The company tops up
statutory pay to 100% of base salary for the first sixteen weeks.

## Sick leave
A self-certificate covers the first three consecutive days. A medical certificate is
required from the fourth day onward.
